# Mission Reflection

Among the three cloud platforms, Google Cloud Platform impressed me the most because of its strong focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes. I found it interesting that each cloud provider has its own strengths, even though they offer many similar services.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform are similar because they all provide computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and security services. They also allow organizations to scale their resources depending on their needs. Even though the service names are different, many of their functions are very similar.

I learned that business requirements are very important when choosing a cloud platform. There is no single cloud provider that is best for every organization. For example, Microsoft Azure is a good choice for organizations that already use Microsoft products, while Google Cloud is suitable for AI and machine learning projects. AWS is also a strong option for businesses that need a wide range of services and global infrastructure.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not only about selecting the most popular platform. It is important to first understand the needs, budget, existing technologies, and future plans of the client. I also learned how to compare similar services across different cloud providers and recommend a solution based on a specific situation.

After completing this mission, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio became more organized and complete. I added research about AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, comparison tables, client recommendations, screenshots, and my KillerCoda Linux investigation. This activity helped me improve both my cloud computing knowledge and my ability to document technical work using Markdown and GitHub.
